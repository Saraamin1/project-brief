Excavation & Trenching Safety Inspection

OSHA Requirement (29 CFR 1926.651): Excavations must be inspected daily by a competent person before work begins and after: rainstorms, other hazard-increasing events, or any indications of possible cave-ins or slides.
⚠ CRITICAL: Protective systems required for all excavations 5 feet or deeper (unless in stable rock). DO NOT ENTER without proper cave-in protection!

1. Protective Systems (Cave-in Protection)

Protective system in place (sloping, shoring, or shielding) for excavations 5+ ft deep
Sloping/benching at correct angle for soil type (Type A: 3/4:1, Type B: 1:1, Type C: 1.5:1)
Shoring/shielding system properly installed per manufacturer specifications
Trench box/shield extends at least 18 inches above excavation floor
No damage, cracks, or deterioration in shoring/shielding components

2. Access & Egress

Ladder, ramp, or steps provided in excavations 4+ ft deep
Access within 25 ft of lateral travel for workers in trench
Ladder secured and extends at least 3 ft above excavation edge
No structural ramps/crossovers used by equipment approved for such use

3. Atmospheric Hazards (Excavations 4+ ft deep)

Atmospheric testing conducted (if hazardous atmosphere possible)
Adequate ventilation provided (if required)
No water accumulation (or pumping equipment in use)

4. Edge Protection & Spoil Piles

Excavation edges barricaded to prevent workers/equipment from falling in
Spoil piles set back at least 2 ft from excavation edge
Materials, equipment kept away from excavation edge (no surcharge loads)
Warning signs posted (e.g., "DANGER - OPEN EXCAVATION")

5. Adjacent Structures & Underground Utilities

Underground utilities located prior to excavation (call 811)
Utilities marked and protected (hand digging within tolerance zone)
Adjacent structures supported/protected as needed (shoring, underpinning)
No signs of stress, movement, or instability in adjacent structures

6. Environmental & Site Conditions

No signs of cave-ins, cracks, or sloughing in trench walls
No water accumulation or seepage into excavation
Inspection conducted after rainstorm or weather event
Soil conditions consistent with initial classification
Vibration or equipment operation not affecting excavation stability
