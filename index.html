Project Information

OSHA Requirement (29 CFR 1926.651)

Excavations must be inspected daily by a competent person before work begins and after: rainstorms, other hazard-increasing events, or any indications of possible cave-ins or slides.

2

Overall Inspection Status

⚠ CRITICAL SAFETY REQUIREMENT

Protective systems are required for all excavations 5 feet or deeper (unless in stable rock). DO NOT ENTER without proper cave-in protection!
